# Ataraxy Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 21, 2026

Ataraxy is a Stoic journal for Android. It is built so that what you write stays on your phone. This policy explains what the app stores, where it goes, and how to remove it.

## The short version

- Ataraxy has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting.
- The app has no internet access at all. It can't send anything anywhere.
- Your journal stays on your phone unless you choose to save a backup somewhere else.

## What the app stores on your phone

Ataraxy keeps the following in its own private storage on your device:

- Your journal: Practice reflections and your morning and evening ritual answers.
- Your What's Yours reflections and the notes you add to them.
- The quotes you bookmark and the feelings you choose in Find Your Footing.
- Your settings, such as theme, text size, reminder times, the birth year you enter for Memento Mori, and whether App Lock is on.
- The style you pick for each home-screen widget.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Android's own cloud backup is turned off for Ataraxy, so your journal is not copied to Google's servers by the system either.

## Backups you make

You can save a backup of your journal, either once or automatically each day, to a file and location you choose with Android's file picker.

- Ataraxy writes the backup only to the place you pick, and reads it only when you restore from it.
- If you choose a folder that belongs to a cloud service such as Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, that service's app uploads the file, and that service's privacy policy covers the copy it keeps. Ataraxy itself never connects to those services.
- Backup files are not encrypted. Keep them somewhere you trust.
- You can turn automatic backup off at any time in Settings, and you can delete backup files yourself.

The readable journal export (a text file in your Downloads folder) works the same way: it is created only when you ask for it, and it is yours to keep or delete.

## App Lock

If you turn on App Lock, Ataraxy asks Android to confirm it's you with your fingerprint, face or screen PIN before showing your journal. That check is done entirely by Android. Ataraxy only learns whether it succeeded and never sees or stores your fingerprint, face data or PIN. For this, the app requests Android's biometric permission.

## Reminders and the widget

- Daily quote and ritual reminders are scheduled on your phone. To show them, the app asks for Android's notification permission. The reminders contain only a quote or a short prompt, never your journal.
- The home-screen widget shows only the day's quote and its author. It never shows anything you have written.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings › Deactivate Ataraxy** erases your journal, reflections, history and settings on the phone.
- **Uninstalling the app** removes everything Ataraxy stored on your phone.
- Backup files and text exports you saved elsewhere are not touched by either. Delete them yourself if you no longer want them.

## Children

Ataraxy is not directed at children under 13.
